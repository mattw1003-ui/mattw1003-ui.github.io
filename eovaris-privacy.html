Eovaris

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

Eovaris is built so that you decide what leaves your iPhone. The developer does not run analytics, show ads, track you or sell data, and does not operate a server that receives your content.

Who we are

Eovaris is developed by Matthew Williams ("we"). Questions about privacy: matthew.williams1003@icloud.com.

What stays on your iPhone

These stay on your device:

  • Chats, action drafts, device projects and settings, kept in the app's private storage with iOS file protection. You choose whether they are included in your device backups (Settings → Data on this device).
  • Speech recognition, document scanning, text recognition from photos, and quick reminders and events are processed on your iPhone.
  • On supported devices, Apple's on-device model creates titles, tidies dictation and writes your briefing without sending anything to OpenAI or Anthropic.
  • Your server connection token is stored in the iOS Keychain on this device only and is never synced.

Your Eovaris server

Chats, agents, shared projects and automations use an Eovaris server that you or your organization operate. When you send a chat or start an agent, the app sends your message, the conversation so far, and the notes, memory and file text of the linked project to that server.

Data stored on your server is controlled by the server's operator, not by us.

AI providers

Your server passes these requests to the AI provider you choose, OpenAI or Anthropic (Claude), to generate a response. The app asks for your consent before anything is shared, and you can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy & AI sharing. Without consent, chats and agents are turned off.

Providers process the data under their own terms: OpenAI privacy policy and Anthropic privacy policy.

Connected accounts

If you connect a Google or Microsoft account on your server, an agent can read the email and calendar information you allow for that run. Sending email, creating events and saving memory always stop for your approval in the app. Sign-in happens with Google or Microsoft directly; the app never sees your password.

Permissions

The app asks iOS for permission before using:

  • Microphone and speech recognition, for voice capture.
  • Camera, for scanning documents.
  • Reminders, to save a reminder you reviewed and approved.

Calendar

Events you approve open in Apple's own event editor, where you confirm them. The app does not read your calendar on this device.

Notifications

If you turn on alerts, the app checks your server and shows local notifications for approvals, questions and finished runs. No push notification service is used, and nothing can be approved from a notification.

Demo mode

Demo mode uses sample data generated on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server or an AI provider.

What we don't do

  • No advertising.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with data brokers.

Children

Eovaris is not directed to children under 13.

Keeping and deleting data

Data on your iPhone stays until you delete it or remove the app. You can delete chats and runs in the app, and deleting the app removes its local data. Data on your server is kept and deleted by its operator; you can delete runs, projects and files from the app.

Changes

If these practices change, this policy and its date will be updated.

Getting started

  • Connect your server: open Settings → Server and enter the HTTPS address and device token from your Eovaris server.
  • No server yet? Tap Try the demo on the connect screen to explore the queue, an agent run and an approval with sample data.
  • Turn on AI sharing when you are ready to use chats and agents (Settings → Privacy & AI sharing).

Do I need a server?

Quick reminders and events, voice capture and document scanning work without one. Chats, agents, shared projects and automations need an Eovaris server with an OpenAI or Anthropic API key.

Will Eovaris send email or create events on its own?

No. When an agent is ready to write anything, it stops and shows you the exact action. You press and hold to approve, or reject it. Drafts become real only when you save or send them.

Why are alerts sometimes late?

Eovaris checks your server while the app is open and during iOS background refresh, which iOS schedules on its own. Opening the app always brings the queue up to date.

How do I delete my data?

Delete chats and runs with a swipe in Agents. Settings → Server → Disconnect removes the connection token. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone.
